about

Kean is a senior at the University of Virginia, studying Cognitive Science with a Computer Science concentration and minors in Data Science and Engineering Business.

His most recent role was at Booz Allen Hamilton, where he worked as a Product Engineer Intern — developing prototypes, product roadmaps, and wireframes, while conducting discovery interviews with Navy stakeholders and synthesizing findings into product insights and requirements.

This past summer, Kean also built a venture inside the iLab Incubator, UVA Darden's ultra-competitive startup accelerator program. As technical Co-Founder, he built the mobile-first app with Next.js 14, React, and TypeScript, then deployed the MVP to Vercel for Demo Night, where the venture drew interest from investors who currently serve as primary stakeholders.

Kean has also worked in sales at a Fortune 500 company, growth at Anthropic, product at Beats by Dre, and engineering at an early-stage startup. His cross-functional experience enables him to drive impact in product and forward-deployed roles where business, technology, user-centered thinking, and interpersonalism intersect.
